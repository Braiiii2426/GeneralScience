How particles move in a solid

Solid particles are packed very closely together. They cannot freely move from one place to another. Instead, they mainly vibrate around fixed positions.

Particle spacing: Very close
Movement: Vibrational motion
L

How particles move in a liquid

Liquid particles remain relatively close together, but they are not locked into fixed positions. They can move and slide past neighboring particles.

Particle spacing: Close together
Movement: Sliding and flowing
