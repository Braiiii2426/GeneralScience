S

How particles move in a solid

Solid particles are packed very closely together. They cannot freely move from one location to another. Instead, they mainly vibrate around fixed positions.

Arrangement: Very close and organized
Movement: Vibrational motion
L

How particles move in a liquid

Liquid particles remain close together, but they are not locked in fixed positions. They can move and slide past neighboring particles.

Arrangement: Close but less organized
Movement: Sliding and flowing
G

How particles move in a gas

Gas particles are much farther apart and move rapidly in many directions. They spread throughout the available space and collide with the container.

Arrangement: Far apart and random
Movement: Rapid and free motion

KEY IDEA

Particles in a solid vibrate in place.

Increasing temperature makes the particles vibrate more strongly, but they remain around their fixed positions.
